💣 Bomb Brawl

Classic four-way bomber battle. The arena plays out on this screen while each player drives their bomber from their phone.

  1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone (2–4 players)
  2. Short on players? Hit 🤖 Add CPU and pick a difficulty
  3. Drag the player list to set starting corners — 1 is top-left, 2 top-right, 3 bottom-left, 4 bottom-right
  4. Set rounds to win, round length and whether power-ups drop, then hit Start!
  5. On their phones: the thumbstick moves, the big button drops a bomb
  6. Bombs blow up in a cross. They smash crates (not steel pillars) and kill anything caught in the flames — including you
  7. Crates hide power-ups: 💣 extra bomb, 🔥 bigger blast, 👟 speed, 🥾 kick (walk into a bomb to punt it down the lane)
  8. Last bomber standing wins the round. If everyone goes up together, whoever fell last takes it — no draws
  9. Run the clock down to 30 seconds and sudden death starts — steel blocks rain down in a spiral until someone dies. First to the target round wins takes the game
