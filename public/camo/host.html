🦎 Camo

Sixteen words on this screen. Everyone shares one secret word from the grid — except the Chameleon, who has no idea which one it is.

  1. Players join by scanning the QR code — 3 to 8 players
  2. Each round shows a topic and 16 words here; phones reveal the secret word privately
  3. In turn, everyone says one word out loud that hints at the secret word
  4. Say too little and you look suspicious; say too much and you hand it to the Chameleon
  5. Discuss, then vote for who you think is faking it
  6. A tie at the top only accuses someone if the Chameleon is in it
  7. Vote wrong and the Chameleon scores 2; catch them and every correct voter scores 1
  8. A caught Chameleon gets one guess at the secret word — nail it and they still take 1
  9. First player to the target score wins
