📝 Category Clash

One random letter, twelve categories, one timer. Write an answer for every category that starts with the letter — but only unique answers score.

  1. Players join by scanning the QR code — no app needed
  2. A letter is drawn at random; the same 12 categories appear on every phone
  3. Everyone races the clock to fill in an answer starting with that letter
  4. "A", "An" and "The" in front of the letter are allowed — nothing else is
  5. Answers are auto-grouped category by category; drag to merge, split, rename, or mark as not counting
  6. An answer scores 1 point if it's valid, starts with the letter, and nobody else wrote it
  7. A fresh letter every round — no letter is ever repeated
  8. Most points after the final round wins
