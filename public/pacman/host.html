👻 Pac-Man Royale

Multiplayer Pac-Man. The maze plays out on this screen while each player steers their Pac-Man from their phone.

  1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone (2–4 players)
  2. No second player? Hit 🤖 Add CPU to add computer Pac-Men
  3. Set the round length and how many round wins take the game, then hit Start!
  4. On their phones: up / down / left / right steer their Pac-Man
  5. Gobble pellets and cherries for points; the maze edges wrap to the other side
  6. A ghost eats you if you're not powered — die and you're out for the rest of the round
  7. Grab a power pellet to turn big and fast: ghosts flee (eat them!) and you can eat smaller players
  8. Highest score among the survivors wins the round — first to the target round wins takes the game
