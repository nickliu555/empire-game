🏟️ Puck Ball

Top-down disc soccer. The match plays out on this screen while every player uses their phone as a live controller.

  1. Players scan the QR code to join from their phone (2–8 players)
  2. Balance the sides — drag a player to reorder them or drop them on the other team (or just tap a name to swap)
  3. Short on players? Hit 🤖 Add CPU to fill a side
  4. Set the match length, then hit Kick off!
  5. On their phones: thumbstick on the left to move, big KICK button on the right
  6. Bump the ball to nudge it; tap KICK while touching it to blast it
  7. The pitch grows with the teams — a tight rink for 1v1, a full stadium for 4v4
  8. When the clock hits zero the match is over — highest score wins
